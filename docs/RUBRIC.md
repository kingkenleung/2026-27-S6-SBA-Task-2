# Marking Rubric — S6 ICT SBA Task 2

**CampusConnect Phonebook · Elective B1: Web Programming and Application**

**Total: 15 marks** · Designed for about 10 hours of student work.

| Criterion | Marks | Excellent | Good | Needs improvement |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Test plan and coverage** | 3 | 15 clear, varied tests covering at least four B1 areas. Steps and expected results are precise. | 10–14 tests with some gaps, repetition or vague expected results. | Fewer than 10 tests, mostly happy-path, or no usable expected results. |
| **Test log, execution and evidence** | 5 | All 15 tests executed. Pass/Fail is accurate. Labelled evidence for every case. Unexpected results are followed up. | Most tests executed with evidence. A few gaps or imprecise results. | Few tests executed; evidence missing or unclear; results inaccurate. |
| **Defect reports and suggestions** | 4 | 3–5 reproducible defects with clear steps, strong evidence and specific, feasible fixes. | Some reproducible defects with reasonable fixes; minor gaps in steps or evidence. | Vague or unreproducible reports; generic fixes such as “improve security”. |
| **Evaluation and high-level design** | 3 | Clear, risk-based evaluation with a release recommendation. Comments on high-level design (for example public IP/domain/HTTPS, architecture, backups, roles) and limitations. | Sound evaluation with a recommendation and some design comments. | Descriptive list of bugs; little or no design comment; no clear recommendation. |
| **Total** | **15** | | | |

## Notes

- **15 test cases** means 15 test plans and 15 test logs; each test has one plan row and one log row.
- **Evidence** can be a labelled screenshot, an HTTP request/response, or a short recording.
- Credit is for systematic testing, clear evidence and useful suggestions — not for finding every bug.
- Strong answers confirm each finding in the running app and explain its impact. Weaker answers report surface symptoms without follow-up.
- The teacher may adjust marks for individual or group work. A short viva can confirm ownership.
- The student brief is `STUDENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` / `STUDENT_INSTRUCTIONS.docx`.
